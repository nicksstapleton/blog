*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

This site (nickstapleton.me) is run by me, Nick Stapleton. I try to collect as little as possible. Here's what happens when you visit.

## Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own, and I don't use advertising or tracking cookies. That's why there's no cookie banner.

## Analytics

I use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't fingerprint you, and doesn't track you across other sites. I only see totals, like page views, referring sites, and countries.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to my newsletter, your email address is stored by [Kit](https://kit.com/privacy) (formerly ConvertKit), which sends the emails. I use it only to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe anytime with the link at the bottom of every email.

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links, mostly for Logos Bible Software. If you click one and make a purchase, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. When you click, you leave this site. The affiliate network (Impact) and the store (Logos/Faithlife) may set their own cookies to credit the sale. Their privacy policies cover that.

## Videos and other services

- **YouTube:** Video pages use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may store information on your device once you press play. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Hosting:** The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), which may log your IP address for security. Some icons load from a public content delivery network, which also sees your IP address. Fonts are hosted on this site.

## What I don't do

I don't sell or share your personal information, and I don't run ads. This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

## Do Not Track

Because this site doesn't track you across other sites, it works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

## Your choices

You can ask me to see or delete any information I have about you, such as your newsletter email. Email me at [nickstapleton@gmail.com](mailto:nickstapleton@gmail.com). If you're in the EU, UK, or a US state with a privacy law, you have these rights, and I'll honor them no matter where you live.

## Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
